About Objects

Brooklyn · since 2019

A small shop with strong opinions about small things.

How it started

Objects began as a folding table at a Sunday flea market in 2019: twelve ceramics, a borrowed lamp and a hand-painted sign. We sold four things that day and talked to about two hundred people. That turned out to be the point.

Today we’re a shop on Kent Avenue and a website, but the job hasn’t changed. Find well-made things with a bit of character, meet the people who make them, and tell you honestly what they’re like to live with.

What we look for

Four rules we mostly follow.

  1. 01

    Used, not displayed.

    If it only looks good on a shelf, it isn’t for us. Everything we stock lives in one of our homes for a month first.

  2. 02

    A little odd.

    We like things that make you smile the second time you notice them, not just the first.

  3. 03

    Made by people we’ve met.

    Thirty-eight makers, most of whom we’ve visited. We know exactly who to call when something chips.

  4. 04

    Built to last.

    Repairable, refillable or simply sturdy. If it breaks, we’ll fix it or tell you who can.

So far

Seven years, roughly.

  1. 2019

    A table at the flea market

    Twelve ceramics, one borrowed lamp, four sales.

  2. 2021

    The shop on Kent Avenue

    A former bike repair shop. It still smells faintly of oil when it rains.

  3. 2023

    Online, carefully

    The website, and a packing table we’re unreasonably proud of.

  4. 2026

    38 makers, 126 objects

    Still small. Still choosy. Biscuit now has a bed under the counter.
